EGG IN A HOLE

Description

Egg in the hole makes a delicious breakfast!

A runny yolk egg cooked in the center of a piece of toast and eaten with a fork is a great way to enjoy an egg in a slightly different way. My kids feel fancy when eating these.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 teaspoons bacon grease
  • 1 slice bread
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Melt bacon grease ina nonstick pan over low heat.
  2. Cut a 2-inch hole in the center of the bread slice.
  3. Place in the hot skillet and cook until lightly toasted, about 2 minutes.
  4. Flip bread and crack egg into the hole.
  5. Season with salt and black pepper.
  6. Continue to cook until egg is cooked and mostly firm.
  7. Flip again and cook 1 minute more.
  8. Serve immediately.
